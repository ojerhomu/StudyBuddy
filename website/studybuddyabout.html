About This App

Learn more about the Study Buddy App.

Description

Study Buddy was created for college students who juggle heavy workloads and need a dedicated space to manage their academic life. Instead of mixing school reminders with personal notifications, the app offers a clean, separate environment designed specifically for class schedules, study plans, and assignments. At the center of the experience is El the Elephant, an AI agent powered by Google Gemini through Firebase. El helps students analyze deadlines, understand time availability, and generate personalized study sessions to stay ahead in their courses.

The app combines everything a student needs into one place: a Pomodoro-driven Study Scheduler, an Assignments Tasks organizer, a smart Calendar for classes and due dates, and a Practice Quiz section where El assists with reviewing course concepts. Study Buddy was built with scalability and security in mind, but more importantly, it was designed to support students in developing better habits, managing stress, and staying academically organized every day.

El the Elephant is your personal AI study companion inside the Study Buddy app. Powered by Google Gemini through Firebase, El assists with analyzing your class schedule, deadlines, and availability to generate optimized study sessions tailored to your needs. El can also quiz you, help you practice course topics, and provide encouragement during Pomodoro study sessions.

Background

Our application uses a client-server architecture to separate the database from the interactive mobile platform. The user interface is designed using Kotlin Android. The architecture of the backend is established in Python using FastAPI to bridge the gap between the two interfaces. Data maintenance is handled by PostgreSQL. The artificial intelligence agent used to create study schedules is installed via Gemini 2.5.

Status

The Study Buddy App is still in development but close to completion! Progress on the program can be followed via the attached GitHub repository.
