About This App

Learn more about the Study Buddy App.

Description

Study Buddy was created for college students who juggle heavy workloads and need a dedicated space to manage their academic life. Instead of mixing school reminders with personal notifications, the app offers a clean, separate environment designed specifically for class schedules, study plans, and assignments. At the center of the experience is El the Elephant, an AI agent powered by Google Gemini.
